Create a visually memorable futuristic hero vehicle for my Unreal Engine cinematic city scene.

This vehicle should appear only occasionally.

It needs to make the viewer briefly wonder:

"What was that?"

The design should feel decades or centuries beyond ordinary vehicles while remaining elegant and believable.

SIZE

Approximately:

Length: 6–8 meters
Width: 3–4 meters

DESIGN

Create an extremely clean dark graphite body.

The upper silhouette should resemble a smooth flattened teardrop or manta shape.

Almost no visible seams.

A narrow smoked-glass passenger canopy should be integrated into the body.

The defining feature is the underside.

Approximately 70% of the underside contains one enormous elliptical levitation aperture.

The aperture should look almost like a hollow glowing ellipse embedded into the vehicle.

The outer edge emits a soft blue-white/cyan light.

The center remains completely dark.

Do not make this resemble a jet engine.

Do not show flames.

Do not show thrust.

Do not show spinning machinery.

The propulsion mechanism should remain unexplained.

MOVEMENT

This vehicle should move much more slowly than expected.

It should glide silently.

The ideal cinematic event is:

The vehicle begins hidden inside heavy fog between distant buildings.

A faint pair of navigation lights appears first.

The silhouette gradually emerges.

The large underside field becomes visible.

The vehicle crosses through a shaft of volumetric light.

For approximately 1–2 seconds, rain running across the hull becomes visible.

It passes behind the primary kiosk or elevated transit line.

It then gradually disappears into fog.

Total visible duration might be approximately 8–15 seconds.

WEATHER EFFECT

As it passes overhead or nearby, create an extremely subtle distortion of rain underneath the levitation aperture.

Rain should curve outward from an invisible field.

A faint mist ring may appear underneath.

The effect should be visible mainly because nearby lights illuminate it.

BLUEPRINT

Create:

BP_HeroVehicle

Use a cinematic spline.

Expose:

TravelTime
SpeedCurve
BankCurve
Visibility/Fog response
Light intensity
Rain displacement intensity

Allow the entire appearance sequence to be triggered from Sequencer.

Please design the Blueprint so the vehicle can be precisely choreographed with Unreal Sequencer while retaining procedural hover and secondary motion.
