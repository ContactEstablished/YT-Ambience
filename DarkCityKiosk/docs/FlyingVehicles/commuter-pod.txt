Create a small futuristic autonomous commuter vehicle for Unreal Engine.

This vehicle sits between a courier drone and a full air taxi.

It should carry one or two passengers.

Approximate size:

Length: 2.5–3.5 meters
Width: 1.5–2 meters

DESIGN

Use an asymmetric design so the viewer cannot immediately compare it to a modern automobile.

One side contains the passenger cabin.

The other side contains a large circular or oval levitation aperture.

Imagine an elegant enclosed passenger pod attached beside a mysterious hollow propulsion ring.

The large ring should NOT resemble a turbine.

It should appear to generate some kind of electromagnetic or gravitational field.

The inner edge of the ring has a very subtle pale cyan glow.

Most of the ring interior remains dark.

The passenger cabin should have heavily tinted curved glass.

Materials:

matte graphite
dark titanium
ceramic black
smoked glass

This craft should look expensive, quiet, and sophisticated.

MOVEMENT

Commuter pods should operate primarily between buildings.

They should feel more maneuverable than larger taxis but not behave like drones.

Suggested movement:

medium speed
smooth banking
gentle elevation changes
occasional short pauses near buildings

They may occasionally rotate slightly in place while waiting for clearance.

Include small random variations in the movement.

One particularly useful behavior:

Allow the vehicle to pause at an invisible "station point" near a building for 5–20 seconds before continuing along the spline.

Make this cinematic rather than a full simulation.
