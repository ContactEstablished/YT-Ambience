I want to replace the conventional quadcopter currently flying through my Unreal Engine futuristic city scene with a much more advanced autonomous courier drone.

DESIGN

Create a compact futuristic courier vehicle approximately 1.0–1.5 meters wide.

The silhouette should resemble a flattened diamond, manta ray, or rounded geometric disc rather than a conventional drone.

The body should be extremely clean and compact:

- no exposed propellers
- no rotor arms
- no landing helicopter-style skids
- no visible conventional jet engine
- no military appearance

Use a dark graphite or black ceramic upper shell.

The underside should contain one recessed oval or circular levitation ring approximately 60–70% the width of the craft.

The levitation ring emits a soft pale cyan-white glow.

The center of the underside should remain dark.

Add:

- one very small forward sensor aperture
- two tiny red navigation lights
- subtle panel seams
- perhaps two tiny articulated stabilization fins

Avoid excessive surface detail.

The design should remain readable even when the vehicle is only 20–50 pixels wide on screen.

PROPULSION EFFECT

The vehicle should appear to hover using an invisible field.

Create a subtle Niagara effect where rain underneath the craft is pushed slightly outward.

Do NOT create a visible rocket exhaust.

Possible effects:

- faint mist displacement
- subtle circular air/rain disturbance
- weak volumetric distortion
- tiny droplets illuminated briefly by the lift ring

These effects should be subtle enough that the viewer may not consciously notice them.

MOVEMENT

Create a reusable Blueprint actor called something similar to:

BP_CourierDrone

The drone should support movement along a spline.

Expose variables for:

Speed
Acceleration
HoverAmplitude
HoverFrequency
YawDrift
LightIntensity
RandomSeed

Suggested behavior:

Cruising speed: moderate-fast
Hover oscillation: approximately +/- 4–8 cm
Hover cycle: 3–6 seconds
Yaw drift: approximately +/- 1–2 degrees
Acceleration and braking should be smooth.

When stopped, the drone should slightly overshoot its destination and softly stabilize rather than snapping into position.

When turning, allow a slight banking motion.

The vehicle should sometimes:

slow down
hover
change altitude slightly
resume flight

Add randomized timing so multiple instances do not move identically.

LIGHTING

The cyan lift ring should softly illuminate nearby fog and rain but should not cast an unrealistically strong pool of light.

This vehicle will often appear high above the street and should feel mysterious rather than visually dominant.

Please give me:

1. Recommended Unreal asset/Blueprint hierarchy.
2. Blueprint logic or pseudocode.
3. Niagara setup.
4. Material setup.
5. Suggested spline movement system.
6. Performance recommendations for approximately 10–30 drones visible in the scene.
